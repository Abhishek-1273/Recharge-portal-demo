1 The 4 Real Challenges to Building the Commercial Live App

1. Telecom API Tie-Ups (Direct Telco vs. B2B Aggregators)

Business Dependency

Telecom operators (Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL) do not provide direct LAPU API connections to new startups without ₹50L+ monthly turnover guarantees.

The Solution: We must partner with a certified B2B telecom aggregator (e.g., Pay2All, Cyrus Recharge, Roundpay, or EzyTM). They provide consolidated APIs for all operators and handle lapu sim server infrastructure.

Action Required: Register a merchant account with 1 or 2 aggregators to get Live API Endpoints + maintain a prepaid liquidity float (₹20,000–₹50,000) in their wallet for processing recharges.

2. Banking KYC & Payment Gateway Production Approval

Regulatory / Legal

Our demo currently uses Cashfree Sandbox (Test Mode). To activate Live Cashfree/Razorpay UPI & NetBanking so retailers can add real bank money into their wallet, payment aggregators mandate statutory business verification under RBI guidelines.

Requirements: Registered Business Entity (Sole Proprietorship, LLP, or Private Limited), Business PAN, GST Registration, and an active Bank Current Account with matching business trade name.

Action Required: Open a Current Account (IDFC, HDFC, or ICICI) under the firm name, obtain GSTIN, and submit business KYC documents to Cashfree for Production Whitelisting.

3. Retailer Experience: Android APK & Bluetooth Printer Integration

Field Usability

Distributors and Super Admins manage operations via web dashboards on laptops, but 95% of Retailers (Dukaan owners) operate exclusively on Android smartphones.

Dukandaars need a fast, lightweight Android app with 4-digit MPIN login (to prevent unauthorized shop boy access), instant push notifications upon recharge completion, and ESC/POS Bluetooth thermal printer connectivity to print customer receipts.

Action Required: Wrap our tested REST APIs into an Android APK (using Flutter or React Native) specifically designed for simple 1-touch mobile recharge and bill collection.

4. Security, SMS OTP Gateway & Automated Reconciler

Tech Infrastructure

To transition from a prototype to production-grade security:

  • SMS Gateway: Integration with DLT-registered SMS provider (e.g., Fast2SMS or MSG91) for retailer phone verification and password resets.
  • Operator Timeout Auto-Reconciler: Operators occasionally hold recharges in "PENDING" for up to 30 minutes. An automated background worker (Cron Job) must poll status every 2 minutes and auto-refund the retailer wallet if the operator fails.
Action Required: Setup TRAI DLT SMS templates for transactional OTPs and deploy a Node.js cron reconciler worker alongside our Supabase Cloud ledger.
